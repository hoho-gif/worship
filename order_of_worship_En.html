Confession

Kneel (Stand)

Officiant
Come, let us bow down and kneel before the Lord our Maker.
People
We kneel before the Lord, the Maker of us all.
Officiant
O Lord, do not rebuke me in your wrath.
People
Do not chastise me in your fierce anger.
Officiant
O God, in your mercy show me favor, and in your great compassion blot out my transgressions.
All
Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin. For I know my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me. Against you, you only, have I sinned, and done what is evil in your sight. Therefore you are justified in your sentence, blameless when you pass judgment. Surely I was sinful at birth; even from my mother’s womb I was sinful. Have mercy on me, O God, according to your steadfast love; according to your abundant mercy blot out my transgressions. Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.
Officiant
Merciful Father,
All
We have done what we ought not to do, and we have left undone what we ought to do. We have sinned in thought, word, and deed. Have mercy on us, who are deeply sinful. Grant us a new life in Christ, that we may walk in your way and glorify your name. Through our Savior Jesus Christ we ask this. Amen.

Officiant
Holy Father, everlasting and all-powerful God, it is always proper and fitting for us to give thanks and praise to the Lord through your Son, Jesus Christ. He is the living Word of the Father. Through him the Father created all things in the beginning, and in his own image made us. The Father sent his Son in human flesh, and through the cross and resurrection he delivered us from the bondage of sin. He was exalted to the Father’s right hand. By the Holy Spirit, you set us before God as your people and made us priests before the Lord to serve all people. Therefore, with the angels and the whole heavenly host, we praise the holy name of the Lord and sing his glory forever.

Nicene Creed

All
We believe in one God, the Father, the Almighty, maker of heaven and earth, of all that is, seen and unseen. We believe in one Lord, Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, eternally begotten of the Father, God from God, Light from Light, true God from true God, begotten, not made, of one being with the Father; through him all things were made. For us and for our salvation he came down from heaven, was incarnate by the Holy Spirit of the Virgin Mary and became truly human. For our sake he was crucified under Pontius Pilate; he suffered death and was buried. On the third day he rose again in accordance with the Scriptures; he ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father. He will come again in glory to judge the living and the dead, and his kingdom will have no end. We believe in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the giver of life, who proceeds from the Father and the Son, who with the Father and the Son is worshiped and glorified, who has spoken through the prophets. We believe in one holy catholic and apostolic Church. We acknowledge one baptism for the forgiveness of sins. We look for the resurrection of the dead, and the life of the world to come. Amen.
Officiant
“The word is near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart.” This is the word of faith that we proclaim. For if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.

Great Thanksgiving

Sit

Officiant
The Lord Jesus Christ, before he suffered, took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to his disciples, saying, “Take and eat; this is my body, which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.”
After supper he took the cup, saying, “Take and drink; this is the cup of the new covenant in my blood, shed for many for the forgiveness of sins. Do this in remembrance of me.”
As often as we eat this bread and drink this cup, we proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.
People
Christ has died; Christ is risen; Christ will come again.
Officiant
We remember the Lord’s life, offered for us in this bread and this wine. We believe in the witness of the resurrection and await the day when the Lord returns to share with us the great banquet of his promise.
People
Amen. Come, Lord.
Officiant
Send now your Holy Spirit, the Spirit of the risen Lord, upon us as we receive the body and blood of Christ, that we may praise your glory and inherit your kingdom with all the saints.
People
Amen. Come, Holy Spirit.
Officiant
To you be all glory and praise, through Christ, with the Holy Spirit, in the Church now and forever.
All
Amen!

The Lord’s Prayer

Officiant
Let us pray as our Lord Jesus Christ taught us.
All
Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, forever. Amen.

Post-Communion Prayer

All
Almighty and everlasting God, we give thanks for the nourishment of this holy communion, by which we have been fed with the precious body and blood of your Son, Jesus Christ. By this grace, you have made us members of his body and heirs of your kingdom. Heavenly Father, we offer ourselves, body and soul, as a living sacrifice to you. Send us by your Holy Spirit into this world to do your will. Glory be to the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, now and forever. Amen.
