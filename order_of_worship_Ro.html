Tsumi no kokuhaku

Kiza (kiritsu)

Shishikisha
Saa, fushiogami, hirefushimashou.
Kaishuu
Watashitachi o tsukurareta kata, Shi no mi mae ni hizamazukimashou.
Shishikisha
Shi yo. Anata no ookina ikari de watashi o semenai de kudasai.
Kaishuu
Anata no hageshii ikidooride watashi o korashimenai de kudasai.
Shishikisha
Kami yo. Mi megumi ni yotte, watashi ni nasake o kake,
Ichidou
Anata no yutakana awaremi ni yotte, watashi no somuki no tsumi o nugui satsute kudasai. Douka watashi no toga o, watashi kara mattaku arai sari, watashi no tsumi kara, watashi o kiyomete kudasai. Makotoni, watashi wa jibun no somuki no tsumi o shitsute imasu. Watashi no tsumi wa, itsumo watashi no me no mae ni arimasu. Watashi wa anata ni, tada anata ni, tsumi o okashi, anata no go me ni akudearu koto o okonaimashita. Soreyue, anata ga senkoku sareru toki, anata wa masashiku, sabakareru toki, anata wa kiyoku araremasu. Aa, watashi wa toga aru mono toshite umare, tsumi aru mono toshite haha wa watashi o migomorimashita.
Shishikisha
Awaremi bukai chichinaru kami yo,
Ichidou
Watashitachi wa, shite wa naranai koto o shi, shinakere ba naranai koto o sezu, omoi to, kotoba to, okonai ni yotte, ooku no tsumi o okashite imasu. Douka tsumibukai watashitachi o o yurushi kudasai. Atarashii inochi ni ayumi, mi kokoro ni shitagai, mi sakae o arawasu koto ga dekimasu you ni, sukuinushi iesu- kirisuto ni yotte onegai itashimasu. Amen

Mi chichi wa, mi ko o hito toshite umaresase, juujika no shi to fukkatsu ni yotte watashitachi o tsumi no kubiki kara kaihou shi, mi ko o go jishin no migi ni ageraremashita. Seirei ni yotte, watashitachi o kami no tami toshite mi mae ni tatase, Shi no saishi toshite Shi to subete no hitobito ni tsukaeru you ni shite kudasaimashita. Mikotoba - senkyou

Kiritsu

Shishikisha
Isuraeru yo kike. Warera no kami, Shi wa yuiitsu no Shi de aru. Watashi wa anata o ejiputo no kuni, dorei no ie kara tsuredashita, anata no kami, Shi de aru.
Ichidou
Anata ni wa, watashi no hoka ni, hoka no kami (kurikaesi) ga atsute wa naranai.
Jibun no tame ni guuzou o tsukutsute wa naranai. Sorera o ogande wa naranai. Sorera ni tsukaete wa naranai.
Anata no kami, Shi no mi na o midarini tonaete wa naranai.
Ansokunichi o oboete, kore o seinaru hi to seyo.
Anata no chichi to haha o uyamae.
Koroshite wa naranai.
Kan'in shite wa naranai.
Nusunde wa naranai.
Anata no tonaribito nitaishi, itsuwari no shougen o shite wa naranai.
Subete anata no tonaribito no mono o hoshigatsute wa naranai.
Shishikisha
Kokoro o tsukushi, omoi o tsukushi, chikara o tsukushi, chisei o tsukushite, anata no kamidearu Shi o aise yo. Anata no tonaribito o anata jishin no you ni aise yo.

Nikea shinjou

Ichidou
Ware wa, yuiitsu no kami, zennou no chichi, ten to chi to subete mi yuru mono to miezaru mono no tsukuri Shi o shinzu. Ware wa, yuiitsu no Shi iesu- kirisuto o shinzu. Shi wa yoyo no saki ni chichi yori umaretaru kami no hitoriko, kami yori no kami, hikari yori no hikari, makoto no kami yori no makoto no kami, tsukurarezu shite umare, chichi to doushitsudeari, banbutsu wa Shi ni yorite tsukurare tari. Shi wa warera jinrui no tame, mata warera no sukui no tame, ten yori kudari, seirei ni yorite otome mariya yori nikutai o uke, hitotonari, warera no tame ni ponteo-pirato no moto ni juujika ni tsukerare, kurushimi o uke, shinite houmurare, seisho ni shitagai sanhime ni yomigaeri, ten ni nobori, chichi no migi ni zashita ma eri. Mata ikeru mono to shinishi mono to o sabaku tame ni, eikou o motte futatabi ki tari tamawan. Sono mikuni wa owaru koto nashi. Ware wa, Shi ni shite inochi o ataeshi seirei o shinzu. Seirei wa chichi to ko to yori i de, chichi to ko to tomoni reihai sare, agamerare, mata yogenmono ni yori te katari tama eri. Ware wa, yuiitsu sei ni shite, ooyake dou naru shito no kyoukai o shinzu. Ware wa tsumi no yurushi no tame no yuiitsu no senrei o shinnin su. Ware wa shisha no yomigaeri to kitarubeki yo no inochi o machinozomu. Amen
Shishikisha
"Mi kotoba wa anata no chikaku ni aru. Anata no kuchi ni ari, anata no kokoro ni aru. "Kore wa watashitachi no nobe tsutaete iru shinkou no kotoba no koto desu. Nazenara, moshi anata no kuchi de iesu o Shi to kokuhaku shi, anata no kokoro de kami wa iesu o shisha no naka kara yomigaerasete kudasatsuta to shinjirunara, anata wa sukuwarerukara desu.

Kansha no inori

Chakuseki

Shishikisha
Seinaru mi kokoro o jouju shi, watashitachi no sukui o nashitogeru tame ni, watashitachi no Shi iesu- kirisuto wa kurushimi o ukeru zenjitsu, pan o tori, kansha shi, kore o saki, deshitachi ni ataete iwaremashita. "Totsute tabe nasai. Kore wa anatagata no tame ni ataeru watashi no karadadearu. Watashi no kinen no tame, kore o okonai nasai".
Shokuji no nochi, sakazuki o mo onaji you ni shite iwaremashita. "Totsute, nomi nasai. Kore wa tsumi no yurushi no tame, anatagata to ooku no hitobito no tame ni nagasu watashi no chi niokeru atarashii keiyakudearu. Watashi no kinen no tame, kore o okonai nasai".
Shi ga kitarareru sono toki made, kono pan o taberu goto ni, kono sakazuki kara nomu tabi ni, watashitachi wa Shi no shi o tsuge shiraseru nodesu.
Kaishuu
Kirisuto wa shini kirisuto wa yomigaeri kirisuto wa futatabi kitararemasu.
Shishikisha
Kono pan to budoushu o motte watashitachi no tame ni sasagerareta Shi no inochi o watashitachi wa kinen shimasu. Shi no fukkatsu no akashi o shinji, Shi ga futatabi kite kuda sari, ooinaru yakusoku no shukuen o watashitachi to tomoni shite kudasaru hi o machinozomimasu.
Kaishuu
Amen. Shi yo, kite kudasai.
Shishikisha
Ima anata no seirei o, fukkatsu no Shi no rei o okutsute kudasai. Shi no kara da to chi to o ukeru watashitachi ga anata no eikou o sanbi shi, subete no seitotachi to tomoni mi kuni o uketsugu koto ga dekiru you ni shite kudasai.
Kaishuu
Amen. Seirei yo, kite kudasai.
Shishikisha
Subete no eikou to sanbi ga, kyoukai nioite, kirisuto ni yori, seirei to tomoni orareru anata ni yoyo kagirinaku arimasu you ni.
Ichidou
Amen !

Ten ni mashimasu warera no chichi yo, negawakuha mina o agamesase tamae. Mi kuni o kitarase tamae. Mi kokoro no ten ni narugotoku, chi ni mo nasase tamae. Warera no nichiyou no kate o kyou mo ataeta ma e. Warera ni tsumi o okasu mono o warera ga yurusugotoku, warera no tsumi o mo yurushita ma e. Warera o kokoromi ni awasezu, aku yori sukuidashita ma e. Kuni to chikara to hae to wa, kagirinaku nanji no mono nareba nari. Amen

Seisango no inori

Ichidou
Eien ni imasu zennou no kami yo, kono seisan ni azukatsuta mono o, mi ko iesu- kirisuto no toutoi karada to chi o motte yashinatsute kudasaru koto o kansha shimasu. Shi wa kore ni yotte, watashitachi ga mi ko no karada no edadeari, mi kuni no yotsugidearu koto o iyoiyo akiraka ni shite kudasaimashita. Ten no chichi yo, watashitachi hamiko ni yotte, kokoro mo karada mo ikita sonaemono toshite sasagemasu. Dou ka, seirei ni yotte watashitachi o konoyo ni tsukawashi, mi mune o okonau mono to narasete kudasai. Eikou wa yoyo ni kagirinaku, chichi to ko to seirei ni arimasu you ni. Amen
